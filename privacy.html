Privacy Policy

DoneVista: Job Photo Reports · Last updated November 17, 2026

DoneVista doesn't collect any data. Your jobs and photos stay on your device and in your own private iCloud. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Who I am

DoneVista: Job Photo Reports ("DoneVista" or "the app") is made by Muhammad Fahid Khan, an independent developer based in Pakistan. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information collected

None. The app doesn't collect, send, sell or share personal data. I never see your jobs, photos, client details or reports.

The app does not:

  • ask you to create an account or sign in
  • send your data to me or to any third party
  • use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking tools
  • use cookies
  • access your location or contacts

What you store in the app

You may enter job details such as client names, phone numbers, email addresses, addresses, notes and photos, and clients may add their name, a rating and a comment when they sign off. This information is stored only on your device and in your iCloud (see below). Please make sure you have your clients' permission to record their details and photograph their property.

Where your data is stored

Everything is stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, your jobs, photos and company settings are also stored in your private iCloud account so they can sync across your devices. Apple runs iCloud under its own privacy policy, and I can't access your iCloud data.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to take before and after photos inside the app. When you add photos from your library, iOS lets you pick specific photos, and the app only receives the ones you choose.

Reports you share

PDF reports are created on your device. They only leave it when you share them through the iOS share sheet, and they go only where you send them.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual jobs, or everything from Settings → Delete All Jobs. This also removes them from your iCloud. Deleting the app removes data stored on the device.

Contacting me

If you email me, I'll use your email address only to reply. If you use Settings → Contact Support, the email also includes your iOS version and app version so I can help with problems. You can remove those lines before sending.

Children

The app is made for businesses and doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top. If the app ever starts collecting data, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.
