Common questions

Where are my jobs and photos stored?

On your device, and in your own private iCloud if you're signed in to iCloud. DoneVista has no accounts and no servers, so I can't see or recover your jobs.

How do I use my jobs on another iPhone or iPad?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices with iCloud turned on. Jobs, photos and your company settings sync automatically. Settings → Backup & Sync in the app shows whether sync is on.

How does ghost alignment work?

When you take the After photo, the Before photo appears on top of the live camera at low opacity. Line the two up and shoot, so both photos share the same angle and zoom. Use the slider to change the overlay strength, or hold Peek to hide it for a moment.

How do I send a report to my client?

Open the job, choose Client Report, then Preview Client Report and tap Share PDF. The job is marked as sent once you finish sharing.

How does client sign-off work?

At the bottom of the report preview, tap Client Sign-Off and hand your device to the client. They add their name, a star rating and an optional comment.

The camera won't open.

Allow camera access in the iOS Settings app under DoneVista → Camera. You can still add photos from your library without it.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete All Jobs removes every job and photo from this device and from your iCloud, so they also disappear from your other devices.
